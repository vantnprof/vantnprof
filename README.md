### ⚛️ About me

- 🔬 I develop **quantum optimization and learning methods** and design **compact hybrid quantum–classical models** using tensor structure, multilinear algebra, and low-rank representations.
- 🧠 My research spans **variational quantum methods, quantum reservoir computing, quantum continual learning, tensor decomposition, and resource-aware quantum–classical execution**.
- 💼 I am seeking **Summer 2027 internships** and **full-time research or engineering roles starting in Fall 2027** in quantum computing and machine learning. [Let's connect](mailto:vantn.prof@gmail.com).
